Purpose. Combine conditions carefully and negate entire logical expressions when you need to exclude selected rows.
Core operators. & means AND, | means OR, and ~ negates a condition.

A Combine conditions

yao.query("age < 18 | age > 65")

yao.query(
  'is_pregnant == "Yes" & is_smoker == "Ex-smoker"'
)
18. “Use & when both conditions must be true.”
19. “Use | when either condition may be true.”

A Query missing values

B Query text patterns

  "occupation.str.contains('Student')"

yao.query(
  "~occupation.str.contains('Student')"
)
